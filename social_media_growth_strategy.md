# Social Media Growth Strategy Review

## Executive Summary

This document provides a framework for reviewing social media accounts and identifying growth opportunities across key platforms.

---

## Platform Analysis

### 1. Twitter / X
**Key Metrics to Track:**
- Follower growth rate (target: +5-10% MoM)
- Impressions and engagement rate (benchmark: 1-3%)
- Reply/retweet/like ratio
- Profile visit → follow conversion rate

**Growth Levers:**
- Post frequency: 3-5 tweets/day optimal for algorithmic reach
- Engage with trending topics relevant to niche within first 30 minutes
- Thread format for high-value content (2-3x more reach than single tweets)
- Reply to accounts with 10x your follower count to borrow their audience

**Action Items:**
- [ ] Audit posting times vs. audience activity windows
- [ ] Identify top 10 performing tweet formats and double down
- [ ] Build list of 50 target accounts to engage with consistently

---

### 2. Instagram
**Key Metrics to Track:**
- Reach vs. impressions ratio (reach/impressions > 0.5 = healthy)
- Story completion rate (target: >70%)
- Reel plays and share rate
- Saves per post (strongest signal for algorithmic distribution)

**Growth Levers:**
- Reels are the primary organic growth driver (3-5x reach vs. static posts)
- Saves > likes for algorithm weight — create "save-worthy" reference content
- Carousel posts get 3x more reach via re-showing to non-engagers
- Hashtag strategy: mix of 5 niche (10K-100K posts), 5 mid (100K-1M), 2 large

**Action Items:**
- [ ] Shift content mix to 60% Reels, 30% Carousels, 10% static
- [ ] Audit hashtag performance — remove tags with <0.5% engagement lift
- [ ] Post 3-5 Stories/day to maintain daily active presence in followers' feeds

---

### 3. LinkedIn
**Key Metrics to Track:**
- Post impressions and engagement rate (benchmark: 2-5%)
- Connection growth vs. follower growth
- Content shares (highest distribution signal)
- Profile views to connection rate

**Growth Levers:**
- Text-only posts still outperform image/link posts for organic reach
- First 60 minutes of engagement critically determines algorithmic spread
- Personal brand posts outperform company page posts by 5-10x
- Commenting on others' posts (10+ substantive comments/day) drives profile visits

**Action Items:**
- [ ] Establish 3 content pillars aligned to professional expertise
- [ ] Implement daily engagement block: 20 min commenting before posting
- [ ] Test document/PDF carousel format (high saves, high reach)

---

### 4. TikTok / YouTube Shorts
**Key Metrics to Track:**
- Average watch time % (target: >50% for algorithmic push)
- Follower-to-non-follower view ratio (higher non-follower = viral potential)
- Share rate (primary virality signal)
- Profile visits after video view

**Growth Levers:**
- Hook in first 1-3 seconds is the single biggest growth lever
- Optimal length: 15-30 seconds for maximum completion rate
- Posting 1-2x/day accelerates learning which content breaks through
- Trending audio increases distribution by 20-40%

**Action Items:**
- [ ] Create 10-video test series on each content format to identify winners
- [ ] Analyze competitor top-performing videos for hook structures
- [ ] Repurpose long-form content into short clips systematically

## Growth Benchmarks

| Metric | Early Stage (<1K) | Growth Stage (1K-10K) | Scale Stage (10K+) |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|-------------------|
| Monthly follower growth | 10-20% | 5-10% | 2-5% |
| Engagement rate | 5-10% | 2-5% | 1-3% |
| Content output | 3-5x/week | Daily | Daily+ |
| Response time | <4 hours | <2 hours | <1 hour |

## 30-60-90 Day Roadmap

### Days 1-30: Foundation
- Audit all accounts, document baseline metrics
- Standardize bio, profile photos, and brand voice across platforms
- Establish content pillars (3-4 topics to own)
- Set up analytics tracking (native + third-party)

### Days 31-60: Experimentation
- Run format tests on each platform (A/B different hooks, lengths, styles)
- Launch consistent engagement routine (comment, reply, DM)
- Identify 2-3 collaboration/cross-promotion partners
- Double down on 1 platform showing strongest early traction

### Days 61-90: Optimization
- Cut underperforming content formats
- Scale what's working: more volume, more budget if applicable
- Launch first campaign or series with documented results
- Set Q2 targets based on Q1 learnings
